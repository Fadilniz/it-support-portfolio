# Zendesk ticket simulation

This is practice, not real work. I used a free Zendesk trial in Oct 2026. All the requesters are fake test users. I did not reply to any real people.

I finished 5 tickets on Oct 24, 2026 and marked them all Solved.

For every ticket I did the same thing: what was reported, what I checked, what fixed it or what I would escalate, then I closed it with an internal note.

## The 5 tickets

### 1. POS terminal frozen mid-order — High, Solved
It froze during dinner rush and orders could not go through.
I checked the power cable, restarted the terminal, and it came back. While it was down I moved orders to the second terminal.

### 2. Receipt printer offline — High, Solved
Orders were coming in but no receipts were printing.
I checked power, paper, and the cable to the POS, restarted the printer, and did a test print. It worked again.

### 3. Staff locked out of POS login — Medium, Solved
A staff member could not log in and could not take orders on that terminal.
I checked the username spelling and caps lock. The account was locked after failed attempts, so I reset the password and tested the login. They could log in again.

### 4. Card reader failing — High, Solved
The reader kept losing connection during payment, so customers could not pay by card.
I checked the connection and cable, restarted the reader, and tested a payment. It worked. If it failed again I would move payments to the second terminal and escalate it.

### 5. Menu price wrong — Low, Solved
The price on the POS did not match the menu board, so customers were charged wrong.
I checked the item name and the right price from the menu board, got manager approval, fixed it on the POS, and tested it. It matched after that.
