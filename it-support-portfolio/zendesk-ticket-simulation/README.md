## How each ticket was worked

For every ticket: report → checks → fix or escalation → internal note → Solved. Priority reflects whether work stopped (High), one person was blocked (Medium), or work could continue (Low).

### 1. POS terminal frozen mid-order during rush — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: POS terminal froze mid-order during dinner rush. Screen stuck, orders cannot be processed.
- Internal note: Checked power cable secure, restarted terminal. Terminal back online after restart. Moved orders to second terminal while it was down.

### 2. Receipt printer offline, orders not printing — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: Receipt printer stopped printing. Orders are coming in but no receipts are printing.
- Internal note: Checked printer power on, paper loaded, cable to POS secure, restarted printer. Test print worked, orders printing again.

### 3. Staff member locked out of POS login — Incident, Medium, Solved
- Reported: Staff member cannot log in to POS. Login keeps failing, so they cannot take orders on that terminal.
- Internal note: Checked username spelling correct, caps lock off, account was locked after failed attempts. Reset password, tested login — staff member can log in again.

### 4. Card reader failing, connectivity drops — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: Card reader keeps failing and losing connection during payment. Customers cannot pay by card.
- Internal note: Checked reader connection and cable, restarted reader, tested a payment. Reader working again. If it failed again, moved payments to second terminal and escalated.

### 5. Menu item price wrong on POS vs menu board — Incident, Low, Solved
- Reported: Menu item price on POS does not match the menu board. Customers are being charged the wrong price.
- Internal note: Checked item name, correct price from menu board, got manager approval for the fix. Price corrected on POS and tested — now matches menu board.
